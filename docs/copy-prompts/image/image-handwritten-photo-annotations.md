## 完整提示词

```text
为照片添加ins风手绘涂鸦排版，保留原照片内容、构图与真实质感。根据画面内容，在空白处添加醒目的白色手写标题和一句简短俏皮的文案，笔画圆润、略带不规则感。搭配少量手绘箭头、星星、闪光、弧线与呼应主题的小图案，以白色为主、其他颜色做点缀。文字与涂鸦自然穿插，活泼随性、疏朗有留白，像亲手记录的生活照片。避开人物面部与重要主体，不堆满装饰，不将照片转成插画，不添加水印。
```

## English Prompt

```text
Add Instagram-style hand-drawn doodle typography to the photograph while preserving its original content, composition and realistic texture. Based on what the picture actually shows, place a prominent white handwritten title and one short playful line in available negative space, using rounded and slightly irregular strokes. Add just a few hand-drawn arrows, stars, sparkles, curved lines and small subject-related motifs, mostly in white with restrained color accents. Let the writing and doodles weave naturally through the photograph, lively and casual yet spacious, like a personal record of everyday life. Avoid faces and important subjects; do not fill every space, turn the photograph into an illustration or add a watermark.
```

使用检查：预览来自作者原帖，非本仓库重新生成；生成标题与短句时应核对事实，避免虚构地点、日期或人物经历。
